The shaders of the app's liquid glass style are adapted from this project's
backdrop/src/commonMain/kotlin/com/kyant/backdrop/internal/Shaders.kt (commit 65ab177): the
rounded-rectangle distance field and its gradient, the rim refraction with its circle map, the
seven-band dispersion and the rim highlight. They were changed: an inner bevel that keeps the
corners round, a lens dome, a surface frost and the corners' gradient. They live in
app/src/main/java/com/github/kr328/clash/ui/components/LiquidGlass.kt. The library itself is not
a dependency of the app.
