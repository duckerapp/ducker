# Roadmap

- **A desktop client** for Windows and Linux.
- **"Only through VPN":** chosen apps get no internet at all while the VPN is off.

No dates yet. An iOS version is not planned for now. It would require a separate app.

What has already been done, version by version, is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/duckerapp/ducker/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md). Ideas and wishes are
welcome in the [Telegram chat](https://t.me/ducker_chat) and
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/duckerapp/ducker/issues).
