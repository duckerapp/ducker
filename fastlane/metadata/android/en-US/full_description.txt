ducker is an Android client for the mihomo core, built on Clash Meta for Android with its own interface.

It is made for one job: add a subscription from your provider, press one button and use the internet. Everything else is one swipe away.

Features:
• Connect in one tap; the home screen shows the server, the subscription term and traffic left.
• Server picker with honest latency checks and a series of measurements for a single server.
• Works with ready-made mihomo configs and with plain lists of vless://, vmess:// and similar links — they are placed into a built-in profile.
• Provider branding: name, logo, announcements, support and renewal links come from subscription headers.
• Stable device id for providers that count devices, and a switch to stop sending it.
• Choose apps that bypass the VPN; your own rules on top of the provider's.
• Home screen widgets, a quick settings tile and intents for Tasker.
• Light, dark and pure black themes.

Privacy: no analytics, no ads, no crash reports sent anywhere. Logs stay on the phone unless you share them yourself, and server addresses and keys are removed first.

Minimum Android version is 10. License: GPL-3.0.
